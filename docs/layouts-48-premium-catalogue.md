## Shared content and route expectations

Each candidate is generated from an independently authored brief and a category-specific composition. The catalogue is expected to include a home page, offering/service page, portfolio or work page, about page, contact page and three illustrative case-study pages. Canonical paths are derived from the category's offering and work route names; candidate definitions and route ownership are validated by the catalogue parser.

The first eight flagship candidates receive additional editorial direction and distinct interior journeys:

- **Meridian:** `/services`, `/work/service-journey`
- **Canvas:** `/process`, `/commissions`
- **Haven:** `/rooms`, `/booking-enquiry`
- **Academia:** `/admissions`, `/student-life`
- **Maison:** `/lookbook`, `/care-guide`
- **Nexus:** `/documentation`, `/integrations`
- **Impact:** `/volunteer`, `/accountability`
- **Estate:** `/viewings`, `/area-guide`

## Acceptance boundary

Registration in this catalogue does **not** make a Layout a purchasable product or approve it for customer installation. Before release, each candidate still requires independent checks for:

1. original artwork provenance and usage rights;
2. accurate, non-deceptive content and removal of fictional claims before customer publication;
3. responsive rendering, accessibility and reduced-motion behaviour;
4. performance and image loading;
5. route safety, tenant isolation and publishing behaviour;
6. editor compatibility, saved-draft recovery and revision conflict handling.

The canonical list is asserted by `src/lib/layout-blueprint/premium-layouts/catalogue-candidates.test.tsx`. That test checks the exact 48 identities, unique IDs, the 47 authored briefs beyond Meridian, candidate parsing, route safety, rendering and flagship journeys. Passing the catalogue test is not equivalent to final release acceptance.
